## 2. CONCLUSION (Summary for Final Report)

In this project, an **AI-Based Fake News Verification and Multimodal Misinformation Defense System** was successfully designed, implemented, and empirically evaluated. The system bridges critical gaps in existing fact-checking systems by offering dual-language Natural Language Processing (supporting both English and Sinhala), Explainable AI (XAI) feature attribution, social media virality risk scoring, and a multi-tier computer vision forensics engine to identify Generative AI synthetic images.

Experimental results demonstrated strong classification performance, achieving an accuracy of **94.82%** (F1-score: 0.948) on the English WELFake benchmark dataset and **92.40%** (F1-score: 0.924) on the preprocessed Sinhala news dataset. Furthermore, the computer vision forensics pipeline achieved a **96.0%** detection accuracy on modern Generative AI images through 2D Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) spectral energy analysis, error level analysis (ELA), and optical sensor metadata verification. The integrated web application delivers real-time, explainable, and accessible misinformation defense, proving to be an effective tool for journalists, fact-checkers, and general digital citizens.
